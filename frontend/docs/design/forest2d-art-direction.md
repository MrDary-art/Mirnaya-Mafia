# Forest 2D v6 — art direction

The Home page is one vertically scrolling forest journey. Eight original 1672 × 941 landscape masters carry the water from a narrow river, through a broad pool and confluence, over a rock cascade, and into a quiet lake. The finale reuses the lake painting. The nine panels are positioned from measured Home section boundaries, overlap by 16% on each side, and use a lower-water crop as a temporary bridge so two separate horizons are not visible at once. The page and paintings scroll together; only the picture planes receive a restrained local parallax (up to 32 CSS px). The existing owl and stump remain on their own transparent WebGL layer.

Visual language: cool pine, slate and river teal, with a small amount of dusk gold. The left side of hero and the relevant text side of each chapter are deliberately quieter and darkened by a local vignette. UI typography, controls and functional cards remain ordinary DOM—not painted into the scenery.

Layer order behind UI: scrolling poster → lower-water bridge at a join → readability vignette → transparent foreground branch → slow distant mist → one viewport-sized Canvas 2D for clipped water highlights, waterfall spray, a single leaf-droplet episode and up to two passing leaves. The owl WebGL canvas is a separate sibling above these; the page DOM is above both. The branch pivots at its upper-left attachment and sways about 0.3–1.2°. Water motion uses elapsed time, never scroll direction, so reversing the page cannot reverse the river. Hero's rare droplet grows at the painted leaf tip, falls toward the water, splashes, then makes a short ripple on a 27-second cycle. Sustained expensive effect frames automatically switch the 2D scheduler to a calmer cadence, with hysteresis before restoring full motion. There is no nature audio.

The desktop and portrait exports are cut from the same masters with individually selected focal points; projection of water polygons, droplet anchors and the image crop is shared in `layoutProjection.js`. The macro-droplet is desktop-only because its leaf tip does not have a safe landing composition in the narrow crop. Scenes outside the current view are browser-lazy-loaded. Product routes reuse the corresponding location as a darker, quieter poster; active conversation, exercise, reading and administration routes suppress all new ambient motion. `full`, `calm`, and `static` are persistent user-selectable modes. Reduced-motion defaults to `static`, can change at runtime, and decorative clocks pause in a hidden tab. If an image or Canvas 2D fails, the dark surface and real DOM remain usable.

Source artwork was generated specifically for this project with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool, using the hero master as the style reference for subsequent scenes. Prompt direction: “original hand-painted 2D temperate forest valley after rain, muted dusk palette, flowing river, natural rock waterfall, cinematic layered depth, quiet left text area, open right area for the existing owl”; later prompts requested the same forest's pool, confluence, cascade, terraces, bend, clearing and lake. The branch was generated as a transparent cutout. No third-party illustration, purchase or external repository data was used. Source PNGs and a separate alpha branch are retained in `frontend/assets/forest2d-source/`; optimized WebP exports come from `scripts/export-forest2d.py` (Pillow). The masters are painted-style generated images, not manually retouched layered PSDs; a human illustration pass could still improve exact shoreline continuity and botanical detail.
