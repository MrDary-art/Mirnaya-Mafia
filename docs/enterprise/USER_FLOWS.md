# Enterprise user flows

## Company representative

1. Open `/business`, review the product, and choose create, login, demo, or request a consultation.
2. Authenticate with the existing Arena account. A new company is created in the same identity system.
3. Complete company setup, departments, and initial practice. Invite team members or share an invitation link.
4. Assign a scenario or program to people or cohorts, set dates and attempts, then review results and audit history.

## Invited employee

1. Open the invitation URL or enter a one-time invitation code in `/business/workspaces`.
2. Sign in or create the existing Arena account; accept the invitation explicitly.
3. Choose a company workspace. `/business/:companyId/me` opens the same company overview as the current workspace home.
4. Review assigned work, start/resume a practice, view released feedback and certificates, and use company directory and notifications within membership scope.
5. Leaving or deactivation removes company access without deleting the personal Arena account.

## Manager, mentor, auditor

- Managers work with the departments, cohorts, and results available to their scope.
- Mentors review assigned team results and comments; they do not become company administrators.
- Auditors have read-only access to allowed analytics/audit data.
- Analytics and benchmarks must suppress cohorts below the configured minimum sample size.

## Current limitations

There is no in-product company messenger or employee self-service archive-sharing flow yet. Scenario publication requires a separate reviewer, but review comments and a dedicated review-history screen are not implemented. Do not claim these missing flows in sales/demo copy as implemented.
